エリアについてAbout the Area

登戸駅は小田急小田原線とJR南武線が乗り入れる、川崎市多摩区の駅です。新宿方面へは快速急行で約15分。「藤子・F・不二雄ミュージアム」（ドラえもんミュージアム）の最寄り駅としても知られ、駅構内にはドラえもんの装飾もあります。Noborito is where the Odakyu Odawara Line meets the JR Nambu Line, in Kawasaki's Tama Ward. Shinjuku is about 15 minutes away via Rapid Express. It's also known as the nearest station to the Fujiko F. Fujio Museum (the official Doraemon museum), and the station itself is decorated with Doraemon characters.
